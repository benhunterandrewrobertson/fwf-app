Help with the Fiordland Wapiti Foundation app for iPhone and Android.

Using the app in the field

  • No coverage? Returns are saved on your phone and upload automatically when you're back in coverage. The Returns tab shows anything still waiting.
  • Offline maps. Before you head in, open the Map tab, tap the offline maps button and download your hunting block (or all of them) while you're on Wi-Fi.
  • Location. Allow location access so returns are pinned where you are. You can also tap the map to move the pin.

Account

  • Forgotten your password? Use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen.
  • To delete your account, see deleting your account and data.
